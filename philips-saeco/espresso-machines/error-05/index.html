Philips / Saeco Error 05: air in the water circuit

Error 05 means: Air is trapped in the water circuit.

Short answer. The pump is running but drawing air rather than water, so no pressure builds. A freshly fitted AquaClean filter that was not primed is the usual cause; an empty tank, a tank not pushed onto its valve, or scale in the inlet are the others.
Part at faultwater circuit / AquaClean filter
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix Error 05

  1. Empty and refill the tank; if you have just fitted an AquaClean filter, shake it for five seconds, hold it upside down in a jug of water until no bubbles rise, then refit.
  2. Push the tank fully home.
  3. Switch on, wait for heat-up, then dispense two or three cups of hot water to purge the circuit.
  4. If the pump still only wheezes, descale; a scaled inlet will not let the pump prime.

When to stop and call a repairer

If the pump runs continuously and never primes after descaling, the pump or a valve has failed.

What it costs

Parts: Nothing for priming; pump $30 to $50.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes.
