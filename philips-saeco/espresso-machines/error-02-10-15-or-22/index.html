Philips / Saeco Error 02, 10, 15 or 22: internal fault

Error 02, 10, 15 or 22 means: Internal fault; Philips lists these as needing service.

Short answer. Philips does not publish what these mean and the manual says contact support. In practice they are the machine failing its self-test on an internal component: a stuck or dead brew-group drive, a pump that is not building pressure, a valve not switching, or a board fault. There is one thing worth trying before you book service.
Part at faultinternal electronics, pump or valve

How to fix Error 02, 10, 15 or 22

  1. Unplug for ten minutes, remove and reseat the brew group, refill the tank and restart. A one-off glitch clears.
  2. Note whether the code appears at startup (self-test, usually the brew group drive or a valve) or when brewing (usually pump or pressure).
  3. If under warranty, contact Philips; they replace rather than repair most of these.
  4. Out of warranty, an independent repairer can test the pump, valve and drive individually.

When to stop and call a repairer

Do not open the machine while under warranty; the seal voids it.

What it costs

Parts: Pump $30 to $50; brew-group motor $30 to $60; board $80 to $150.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? On a LatteGo 3200/5400 or Xelsis, yes; on a ten-year-old Saeco, price a replacement.
