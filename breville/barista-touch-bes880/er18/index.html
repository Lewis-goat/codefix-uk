Sage / Breville Barista Touch ER18: electrical safety protection triggered

ER18 means: E-fast protection triggered due to a detected electrical or safety fault.

Short answer. The machine detected an electrical fault: leakage current to ground, an unexpected mains waveform, or a heater drawing power when it should not. Water on a heating element or the power board is the everyday cause. This is the code that also trips the GFCI / RCD in your kitchen.
Part at faultmains electrical / triac board
Severityhigh

How to fix ER18

  1. Unplug. Do not keep resetting the breaker; each trip is the machine telling you current is going where it should not.
  2. Let the machine dry for 24 hours somewhere warm. A machine that has been descaled or has a slow leak often has water around the element terminals.
  3. Check the mains cable and plug for damage and try a different socket on a different circuit.
  4. If it runs after drying, find and fix the leak (o-rings, hoses, boiler fittings) or it will return.
  5. If it trips again dry, the element insulation or the power board has failed. That is a technician job.

When to stop and call a repairer

This is a mains-electricity fault. Unless you are competent with live testing, hand it to a repairer.

What it costs

Parts: Triac / power board $80 to $150; heating element $60 to $150.

Service: Out-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.

Worth repairing? Drying out: yes. Element or board on an old machine: get a quote first.

How to check the error log

Switch the machine off at the wall. Hold the front Power button, switch the wall power back on and release when the logo appears. Enter the service password 00000, then open Error Counter to see stored faults or Live Debug for live temperatures.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
