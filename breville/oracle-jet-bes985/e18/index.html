Sage / Breville Oracle Jet E18: tamping / dosing motor fault

E18 means: Auger motor overcurrent detected due to mechanical resistance or jam.

Short answer. The auto tamper or dosing auger did not finish its travel in the time allowed. Dried coffee oils on the tamper head or in the auger, a portafilter not seated in the cradle, or a very fine grind that packs hard are the usual reasons.
Part at faultauger (dosing) motor
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany time

How to fix E18

  1. Remove the portafilter and wipe the tamper head and cradle with a dry cloth; oily buildup makes the tamper stick.
  2. Check the tamp fan and auger are fitted correctly to the drive shaft after any cleaning.
  3. Run the tamp calibration routine from the settings menu if your model has one.
  4. Coarsen the grind slightly and retry.
  5. If the tamper jams mid-stroke every time, the assembly has failed and is replaced as a unit.

When to stop and call a repairer

Keep fingers clear of the tamper cradle while it moves.

What it costs

Parts: Nothing for a jam; tamp motor assembly about $80 to $130.

Service: Out-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.

Worth repairing? Yes.

How to check the error log

Open Settings on the touch screen, then About the Machine, then Software version. On that screen tap each bottom corner of the screen several times. Go back to About the Machine and a new Service center mode entry appears. Open it to view the error log.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
