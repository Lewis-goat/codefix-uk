Sage / Breville Barista Touch Impress error codes

18 codes. The Barista Touch Impress has single ThermoJet heater; most codes are one of its temperature sensors, a water-level or flow fault, or the grinder.

The Impress shares its control board family and code table with the Barista Touch BES880, so fixes are the same.

How to read the stored error log

Unplug the machine. Press and hold the Power button while plugging it back in. When the password screen appears, enter 02015 to open Service Mode, then open the error log.

Service menus are intended for technicians. Reading the log is harmless; do not change calibration values.
