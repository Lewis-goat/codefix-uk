Sage / Breville Oracle Touch ER28: internal communication fault

ER28 means: Communication fault between control boards.

Short answer. The display or main board has lost contact with another board inside the machine. Nine times out of ten that is a ribbon cable that has worked loose, moisture on a connector, or a board that has been splashed by a leak. A genuinely dead board is the rarer, dearer case.

How to fix ER28

  1. Unplug for ten minutes, then power back on. Board resets clear a surprising number of these.
  2. Look for a leak. Pull the drip tray, tank and hopper and look for water trails under the machine or on the base plate.
  3. Open the machine and reseat every ribbon cable and plug between the display board, main board and power (triac) board. Push each connector home until it clicks.
  4. Dry any wet connector with a hair dryer on low, and fix the leak that wet it.
  5. If the fault persists with everything dry and seated, one of the boards has failed. Get a quote before ordering: board prices vary a lot by model.

When to stop and call a repairer

If the display is blank or flickering as well, do not keep power-cycling; that can take a struggling board from intermittent to dead.

What it costs

Parts: Nothing if it is a cable; a control board is $100 to $250.

Service: Out-of-warranty Breville service quotes for internal faults are commonly $300 to $500, sometimes more, plus shipping. Independent espresso repair shops are usually cheaper for a sensor swap.

Worth repairing? Cable or moisture: yes, free. Board: compare the quote with a refurbished machine.

How to check the error log

Switch the machine off at the wall. Hold the front Power button, switch the wall power back on and release when the logo appears. Enter the service password 00000, then open Error Counter (stored faults) or Live Debug (live temperatures and water levels).

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

When does ER28 typically show up?

Sage / Breville Oracle Touchs report ER28 any time. There is no reliable pattern to when it appears — treat any occurrence as real.

Can I keep using the machine with ER28 showing?

Treat it as a stop-using signal: ER28 marks a fault that can damage the machine (or worse) if you keep running it. Do the safe first steps below, then stop.

Will ER28 clear on its own?

Sometimes. Try the reset and cleaning steps first — they cost nothing and clear the milder cases. A code that returns within a day or two needs the parts fix.

Thirty minutes to an hour if you are comfortable opening the case; budget an afternoon the first time.
