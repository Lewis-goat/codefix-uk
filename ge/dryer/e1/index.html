GE Appliances dryer E1: door switch fault

E1 means: Door switch / latch fault.

Short answer. The board does not see the door as closed. A worn latch, a switch that has failed, or a loose wire on it.
Part at faultdoor switch
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix E1

  1. Close the door firmly and press Start.
  2. Check the latch strike is not bent or broken.
  3. Unplug, remove the front panel and check the door switch plug; replace the switch if it does not click.

When to stop and call a repairer

Unplug before opening the panel.

What it costs

Parts: Door switch $10 to $25.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Yes.

How to check the error log

On newer GE dryers (GTD/GFD 2016 on), with the dryer off, press and hold the Signal and Temp buttons for 5 seconds to enter service mode; the last stored error shows. On SmartHQ models the app lists stored codes. Unplugging for 5 minutes resets the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
