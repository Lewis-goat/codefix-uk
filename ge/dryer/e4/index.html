GE Appliances dryer E4: no heat: thermal fuse

E4 means: Thermal fuse blown or high-limit cut-out tripped: no heat.

Short answer. The dryer runs but does not heat because a safety fuse has opened. Fuses blow because airflow was restricted (lint, a blocked vent) and the heater overheated; fix the airflow or the new fuse blows too.
Part at faultthermal fuse / high-limit thermostat
Severitymedium
DIY difficultymoderate
When it appearsany

How to fix E4

  1. Clean the lint filter and the whole exhaust run to the outside.
  2. Unplug, open the cabinet and meter the thermal fuse and high-limit thermostat on the heater housing; replace whichever is open.
  3. Run a cycle with the vent disconnected briefly to confirm heat, then reconnect.

When to stop and call a repairer

Never bypass a thermal fuse.

What it costs

Parts: Thermal fuse $5 to $15; high-limit thermostat $10 to $25.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Yes.

How to check the error log

On newer GE dryers (GTD/GFD 2016 on), with the dryer off, press and hold the Signal and Temp buttons for 5 seconds to enter service mode; the last stored error shows. On SmartHQ models the app lists stored codes. Unplugging for 5 minutes resets the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
