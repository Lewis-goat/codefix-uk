GE Appliances dishwasher C1: drain timeout

C1 means: Drain pump timeout: the pump ran more than 2 minutes without draining (older models: stuck key).

Short answer. The dishwasher tried to pump out and the water did not go. Clogged filter, a blocked or kinked drain hose, a new garbage disposal with the knockout plug still in, or a drain pump that has failed.
Part at faultdrain pump, filter and drain hose
Severitymedium
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix C1

  1. Remove the lower rack and clean the filter and sump; pull out food debris and glass.
  2. Check the drain hose under the sink for a kink and, if a disposal was fitted recently, that its knockout plug was removed.
  3. Cut power for 60 seconds at the breaker to reset and run a drain-only cycle.
  4. If it still will not drain, the drain pump impeller is jammed or the pump has failed; it is accessible from inside the tub on most models.

When to stop and call a repairer

If water is leaking under the machine, stop and find the leak first.

What it costs

Parts: Drain pump $40 to $80.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Yes.

How to check the error log

On most GE dishwashers, hold Start for 5 seconds with the door open to enter the service menu; on models with a hidden display, press Select Cycle and Start together for 5 seconds. The last error shows on the display. Turn power off at the breaker for 60 seconds to reset the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
