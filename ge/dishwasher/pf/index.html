GE Appliances dishwasher PF: power failure

PF means: Power failure during a cycle.

Short answer. Power was interrupted mid-cycle. Nothing is broken.
Part at faultmains supply
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix PF

  1. Press Start to resume or cancel.
  2. If it happens with no outage, check the breaker and the plug under the sink.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous here.

What it costs

Parts: Nothing.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Yes.

How to check the error log

On most GE dishwashers, hold Start for 5 seconds with the door open to enter the service menu; on models with a hidden display, press Select Cycle and Start together for 5 seconds. The last error shows on the display. Turn power off at the breaker for 60 seconds to reset the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

FAQ

What does PF mean on the GE Appliances dishwasher?

Power failure during a cycle. Power was interrupted mid-cycle. Nothing is broken.

Can I fix PF myself?

DIY difficulty: easy. Press Start to resume or cancel. If it happens with no outage, check the breaker and the plug under the sink.

How much does it cost to fix PF?

Nothing. A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Is it worth repairing?

Yes.

PF during startup or during use?

GE Appliances dishwashers report PF any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with PF showing?

Generally yes — PF is more of an annoyance than an emergency. Fix it when convenient; just do not let the cause sit for months.

Will PF clear on its own?

Often, yes. The first step (press start to resume or cancel) clears a share of cases on its own. If the code comes straight back, the part is genuinely at fault.

How long does the repair take?

Fifteen to thirty minutes with basic tools, once you have the part.
