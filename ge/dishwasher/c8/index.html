GE Appliances dishwasher C8: dispenser blocked

C8 means: Detergent cup blocked / dispenser did not open (on some models: water still too cold).

Short answer. The dispenser door could not open, usually because a dish or pan is in front of it, or dried detergent has jammed the latch.
Part at faultdetergent dispenser
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix C8

  1. Load so nothing blocks the dispenser door.
  2. Clean the dispenser and latch with hot water.
  3. Replace the dispenser assembly if the latch is broken.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous here.

What it costs

Parts: Dispenser $30 to $60.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Yes.

How to check the error log

On most GE dishwashers, hold Start for 5 seconds with the door open to enter the service menu; on models with a hidden display, press Select Cycle and Start together for 5 seconds. The last error shows on the display. Turn power off at the breaker for 60 seconds to reset the board.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
