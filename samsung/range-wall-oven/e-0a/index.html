Samsung range and wall oven E-0A: oven overheating

E-0A means: Oven overheating.

Short answer. The oven got hotter than set. Either the sensor is under-reading or a relay on the board has stuck closed and the element will not switch off.
Part at faultoven temperature sensor / control board relay
Severityhigh
DIY difficultymoderate
When it appearsany

How to fix E-0A

  1. Cut power at the breaker immediately.
  2. After cooling, meter the sensor (about 1080 ohms at room temperature; replace if far off).
  3. Inspect the control board for burnt relays; replace the board if the oven overheats with a good sensor.

When to stop and call a repairer

A stuck relay means the element will not turn off. Do not use the oven until fixed.

What it costs

Parts: Sensor $20 to $40; relay board $100 to $200.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Sensor: yes. Board on an old range: compare quotes.

How to check the error log

Samsung ovens do not have a user-accessible error log. The code stays on the display until you fix the cause or cut power at the breaker for 3 minutes; if it returns after a reset, treat it as real.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.
