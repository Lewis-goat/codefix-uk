Samsung range and wall oven cE-56: control board fault

cE-56 means: Internal control board fault.

Short answer. The board failed its own check.
Part at faultcontrol board
Severityhigh
DIY difficultyhard
When it appearsany

How to fix cE-56

  1. Cut power for 3 minutes.
  2. Reseat all board connectors (power off).
  3. Replace the board if it returns.

When to stop and call a repairer

Cut power first.

What it costs

Parts: Board $100 to $250.

Service: A home visit from an appliance technician runs $120 to $250 for diagnosis plus the part. Boards are the expensive part; sensors and switches are cheap.

Worth repairing? Depends on age.

How to check the error log

Samsung ovens do not have a user-accessible error log. The code stays on the display until you fix the cause or cut power at the breaker for 3 minutes; if it returns after a reset, treat it as real.

Clear the log after a repair so you can tell whether the fault has really gone.

cE-56 during startup or during use?

Samsung range and wall ovens report cE-56 any. That timing is a useful clue when narrowing the cause.

Can I keep using the machine with cE-56 showing?

Treat it as a stop-using signal: cE-56 marks a fault that can damage the machine (or worse) if you keep running it. Do the safe first steps below, then stop.

Will cE-56 clear on its own?

Rarely. A reset or power cycle is worth one try, but cE-56 with a "hard" difficulty rating almost always means a failed component that has to be replaced.

How long does the repair take?

This one is a bench repair for most owners — the teardown, test and reassembly are the bulk of the work, not the part itself.
