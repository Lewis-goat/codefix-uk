Jura Empty drip tray (tray is empty): false drip-tray message

Empty drip tray (tray is empty) means: Tray message will not clear, or "Tray missing" with the tray fitted.

Short answer. The tray is detected and its fullness measured through two metal contact strips on the back of the tray. Coffee residue, milk or scale bridging them reads as full; corrosion or a bent strip reads as missing.

How to fix Empty drip tray (tray is empty)

  1. Remove the tray, wash it, and wipe the two metal strips dry with a damp cloth, then dry.
  2. Wipe the mating contacts in the machine.
  3. Refit the tray firmly until it clicks.
  4. If it still reads full, the strips are corroded through; a new tray is the fix.
