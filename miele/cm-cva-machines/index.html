Miele coffee systems (CM 5510/6150, CVA 6401/6805) error codes

Miele coffee systems show F-codes from the built-in self diagnosis. The water-supply codes are user-fixable; the valve and brew-unit codes usually need Miele service.

Meanings below follow the Miele operating-instruction manuals; wording varies slightly between CM countertop and CVA built-in models.

All codes

CodeMeaningFaultDIY
F10 / F17No water, or very little water, is being drawn in — the Miele manual wording for a water-supply faultwater not drawn ineasy
F77Internal fault, most often at valve initialization — the Miele manuals route it to a power cycle, then servicevalve initialization faultnot recommended
