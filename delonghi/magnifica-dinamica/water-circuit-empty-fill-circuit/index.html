De'Longhi Magnifica / Dinamica Water Circuit Empty / Fill Circuit: air in water circuit

Water Circuit Empty / Fill Circuit means: The machine cannot detect water in the circuit; air is trapped or the pump is not priming.

Short answer. Air has got into the pump, usually after the tank ran dry, after descaling, or after a new filter. The pump cannot push air, so pressure never builds and the machine asks you to fill the circuit.
Part at faultpump, tank valve and water circuit
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy
When it appearsany

How to fix Water Circuit Empty / Fill Circuit

  1. Fill the tank at least halfway, remove the water filter for now, and push the tank fully home.
  2. Fit the hot-water spout and press the hot water button; let it run until water flows steadily (may take two or three attempts).
  3. Refit the filter after priming it in water.
  4. If the pump buzzes but no water arrives after several attempts, descale; a scaled inlet or heater will not let it prime.

When to stop and call a repairer

If the pump is silent, it has no power or has failed.

What it costs

Parts: Nothing for priming; pump $30 to $50.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes.
