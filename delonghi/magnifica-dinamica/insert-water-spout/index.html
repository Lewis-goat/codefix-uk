De'Longhi Magnifica / Dinamica Insert Water Spout: spout not detected

Insert Water Spout means: Machine does not detect the hot-water / milk spout when it needs to run a rinse or relieve pressure.

Short answer. The machine wants the spout on (to rinse, descale, or relieve pressure) and its sensor cannot see it. The spout is not pushed fully on, its O-rings are worn so it sits loose, or the sensor magnet/contact is dirty.
Part at faultspout connection and its sensor

How to fix Insert Water Spout

  1. Push the spout on fully; it should click or seat with resistance.
  2. Check the O-rings on the spout stub; replace if flattened.
  3. Clean the spout and the socket; scale here is common.
  4. If the message appears with "Ground too fine", descale the machine.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous here.

What it costs

Parts: O-ring kit $5 to $10; spout $20 to $40.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes.
