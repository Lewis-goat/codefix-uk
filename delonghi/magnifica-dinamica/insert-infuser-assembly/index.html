De'Longhi Magnifica / Dinamica Insert Infuser Assembly: infuser not seated

Insert Infuser Assembly means: Infuser not detected in its seat.

Short answer. The infuser has been refitted after cleaning but is not fully home, or something (a stray puck, a chip of plastic) is stopping it seating. The machine will not run without it.
Part at faultinfuser assembly
Severitylow
DIY difficultyeasy

How to fix Insert Infuser Assembly

  1. Remove the infuser and check the cavity for debris; use tweezers for anything stuck in the rails.
  2. Make sure the infuser lever is pressed down and the piston is in its rest position (press the top until it clicks).
  3. Slide it in on the guides and push until it clicks, then close the door.
  4. If the message persists, unplug for a minute and try again; then inspect the infuser for a broken tab.

When to stop and call a repairer

Nothing dangerous here.
