De'Longhi Magnifica / Dinamica Leak alarm (code 3963): internal leak

Leak alarm (code 3963) means: Water detected in the base of the machine.

Short answer. A sensor in the base has seen water. The usual leak points are the tank valve O-ring, the infuser seals, the heater O-rings and the drainage valve.
Part at faultseals, tubes and boiler fittings
Severitymedium
DIY difficultymoderate
When it appearsany

How to fix Leak alarm (code 3963)

  1. Unplug. Empty the drip tray and dry the base; look where the water was.
  2. Check the tank valve O-ring and the tank seating.
  3. Remove the infuser and inspect its seals and the outlet.
  4. If water is inside the housing, open it and trace the wet path: usually a heater O-ring or a split tube.

When to stop and call a repairer

Water and the power board share the base; do not run it until dry and fixed.

What it costs

Parts: O-ring kits $5 to $15; drainage valve $10 to $20.

Service: Out-of-warranty manufacturer service for a super-automatic is typically $250 to $500 including return shipping; independent espresso repairers are usually cheaper for a single part.

Worth repairing? Yes.
